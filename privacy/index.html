Hole Arcade privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026.

Hole Arcade is a game you play on your own device. This page says what the game and its ad and payment partners do with information. The contact email below is a placeholder until the developer publishes a real address.

No account

Hole Arcade does not ask you to create an account. It does not ask for your name, email address, or phone number. Progress, coins, music, sound effects, haptics, and the Remove Ads flag are stored on the device.

Ads

The free game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob can fill those ads itself or through mediation, which means other ad networks Google works with. Those partners may collect an advertising id, device model, language, coarse location derived from the IP address, and how you interact with an ad. They use that to show and measure ads. On iPhone, the game asks permission before tracking (App Tracking Transparency). You can say no and keep playing. Remove Ads turns off interstitial ads. Rewarded ads stay optional.

The current test build uses Google's sample ad unit ids. A store build will use the developer's own AdMob ids after that account exists.

Purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time $2.99 purchase. On Android, Google Play Billing handles the payment. On iPhone, Apple In-App Purchase handles it. Hole Arcade does not see your card number. The store tells the game that the purchase succeeded, and the game remembers that on the device. The store keeps the receipt under your store account.

Local saves

Saves stay on the device (app storage on a phone, or the browser's local storage on the web test build). They are not uploaded to a Hole Arcade server. There is no Hole Arcade server for player data. Clearing the app's storage, or the site data for the web build, deletes the local save.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information. We do not ask for precise location, contacts, photos, or messages. There is no chat and no public profile.

Children

The game is a cartoon eating game. It is not a social network. Ads and the purchase are described above. A parent can turn off personalized ads in the device settings and can refuse tracking on iPhone.
