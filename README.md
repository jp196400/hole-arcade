# Hole Arcade

A Godot 4.3 hole.io-style game. Steer with a floating joystick, swallow what fits when the hole is under it, and clear 10 themed maps. Each map covers about ten times the area of the first street grid, with the same density of buildings, vehicles, and small props split across districts so a theme does not repeat as one pattern. Chunks off-screen are culled, small props fade on far chunks, and physics runs only on objects next to a hole. Coins, unlocked levels, stars, skins, the selected skin, upgrades, lifetime challenge stats, sound and music, Remove Ads, and the daily reward are saved in the browser (IndexedDB, with a localStorage backup of whichever copy is newer). Safari can clear that site storage after about 7 days without a visit. A native iOS or Android build keeps the same file on the device and does not have that limit. The game opens straight into the saved level. A Drag to move hint shows on the first round. Ads and Remove Ads are test placeholders, with no ad account and no charge.

Play it at https://jp196400.github.io/hole-arcade/

The bottom-left corner shows the build id (the first 7 characters of the commit). The page loads `index.js`, `index.wasm`, and `index.pck` with `?v=<commit>` so a refresh cannot keep an older pack. The Web preset exports with thread support off. UI text draws at the phone's native resolution. The 3D view starts at 1.5× CSS pixels with 4× MSAA, divided by the screen's pixel ratio, then steps that CSS scale between 1.0 and 2.0 during the first seconds so the frame rate stays near 60. If 1.0× is still short, it drops to 2× MSAA plus FXAA. The hole rim is a shader circle with analytic edge smoothing, so the opening stays round at every size. A level ends when the map is about 95% cleared (eaten by you or a rival). The last stretch marks the nearest leftovers. The hole grows steadily across that clear, to about eight times its starting radius, so the largest object fits by the end. Each size step pops the hole and shows the new size, from Size 1 through Size 7. The camera eases out more slowly than the hole grows, so the opening goes from about a quarter of the screen width to about 40%. A bigger rival that swallows you opens the results screen, with a Retry button and one rewarded continue. Add `?debug=1` for FPS and draw calls, `?speed=0.5` to scale movement, or `?unlockall=1` to open every level and skin.

Only Downtown is open at first. The next map opens after a 3-star clear of the previous one and three more full clears for each step, so a 2-star clear does not open it. Stars are 1 for a half-cleared run, 2 for a clear, and 3 when you personally eat at least 45%. The maps are Downtown, Suburbs, Park, Boardwalk, Farm, Harbor, Campus, Fairground, Industrial, and Skyline, each with its own layout and sky. Cyan is the only skin that starts unlocked. Each other skin has its own saved challenge, from a clear without being eaten and three clears in a row up to 7,000 cars, 3,500 trees, 1,400 buildings, 120 rival holes, and a full clear of every level. The skin menu shows the challenge and a progress bar, and a toast appears when one is completed. A locked skin can be tried for one round after a rewarded ad. A save from an older build keeps skins and maps it already had. Add `?unlockall=1` to open every level and skin.

`FIRST_AD_SECONDS` is 120: the first interstitial waits until two minutes of cumulative play, then pauses the round. Placeholder interstitials show Skip after `SKIP_AFTER_SECONDS` (5). A later AdMob build would let the ad network time that skip. After the first round, a skippable interstitial can appear between rounds on a 2-then-3 round cadence, and never sooner than 90 seconds after any ad. Rewarded placeholders must finish to pay out; the X forfeits. Those offers are: watch an ad for 2x coins, watch an ad to keep going once per round after being eaten, try a locked skin, and double the daily reward. There is no banner during play. Remove Ads is a stub priced with `REMOVE_ADS_PRICE` at $2.99. It turns off interstitials only.

Buildings, roads, cars, people, and trees are [Kenney](https://kenney.nl) CC0 models (City Kit roads, commercial, and suburban, Car Kit, Mini Characters, Nature Kit). Smaller props are original low-poly meshes. See `assets/kenney/LICENSE.txt`. The music loop is an original 76 BPM calm piece released under CC0 (`assets/music/LICENSE.txt`). Sound effects are original. The HUD uses [Fredoka](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Fredoka) under the SIL Open Font License (`assets/fonts/OFL.txt`).

Open the project in Godot 4.3 and run `main.tscn`. Pushes to `main` export the Web preset and deploy `build/web` to GitHub Pages.
