## Google Play full description

Hole Arcade is a top-down eating game. You are a hole in the ground. Drag to roll through town, swallow what fits, and grow until the street itself is a snack.

Ten maps, each with its own streets and props: a farm valley, a candy town, a dinosaur jungle, a pirate harbor, a snowy village, a desert oasis, a toy room, a space colony, a haunted hollow, and a theme park. Clear a map by eating enough of it. Rival holes are eating too.

A hole can swallow another hole only when it is a little bigger. Green means you can eat them. Red means they can eat you. Rivals grow from their own bites, slower than a player who keeps eating, so a busy round can pull you ahead.

Drag anywhere to move. Your hole starts small, so cones, crops, and crates go first. Buildings, vehicles, and trees wait until you are wide enough. Fill the clear bar to finish the map.

Progress, coins, and settings stay on the device. There is no account.

The free game shows interstitial ads between rounds through Google AdMob, which may include mediation partners. Rewarded ads are optional. Remove Ads is a one-time $2.99 purchase that turns interstitials off. Rewarded ads stay available.

Music is a CC0 loop. Sound effects are original.

## App Store full description

Hole Arcade is a top-down eating game. You are a hole in the ground. Drag to roll through town, swallow what fits, and grow until the street itself is a snack.

Ten handmade towns are waiting: a farm valley, a candy town, a dinosaur jungle, a pirate harbor, a snowy village, a desert oasis, a toy room, a space colony, a haunted hollow, and a theme park. Eat enough of the map to clear it. Other holes are out there with you.

You can swallow a rival only when your hole is a little bigger. Their name and ring turn green when you can eat them, and red when they can eat you. Keep eating and you can open that lead. They grow, just not as fast as you do while you are the one clearing the street.

Drag anywhere. Start on the small snacks. Come back for the buildings, cars, and trees when the hole is wide enough.

The game stores progress on this device. There is no account to sign in to.

Ads are served by Google AdMob and may include mediation partners. The game asks before tracking, and you can say no. Remove Ads is a one-time $2.99 in-app purchase that turns interstitial ads off. Rewarded ads stay optional.

Music is a CC0 loop. Sound effects are original.
